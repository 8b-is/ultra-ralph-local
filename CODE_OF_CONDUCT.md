# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, specific, and constructive.

## Expected behavior

- assume good intent
- give actionable feedback
- keep discussions technical and civil
- do not post secrets or private data

## Unacceptable behavior

- harassment
- personal attacks
- doxxing
- intentionally posting credentials or sensitive logs

Project maintainers may remove content or block participation when needed to keep the project safe and usable.
